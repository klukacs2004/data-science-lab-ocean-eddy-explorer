Aspect Paper This explorer
Input data Absolute SSH/ADT and geostrophic velocities in the Mozambique Channel; altimetry sampled every seven days. Daily OSU U.S. West Coast SLA and supplied geostrophic velocity. This is an SLA adaptation, not an absolute-SSH reproduction.
Filtering No SSH high-pass filtering. Two Hanning passes on W. Height is unchanged. Configurable W smoothing, defaulting to two passes; finite-neighbour normalization preserves missing cells.
Contours 2 cm intervals; maximum diameter 600 km. Same defaults, adjustable for exploration. Diameter is the maximum great-circle distance between contour vertices.
Hybrid selection Combine negative W with closed SSH loops to reduce noise and multi-core ambiguities. Eight-connected W < 0 regions, represented by their minimum-W grid point. Require one enclosed representative and retain the largest eligible loop per core. These are explicit reconstruction choices for details not fully specified by the article.
Data guards Operational boundary details are not fully specified. Open and oversized contours are excluded before the candidate table. Missing interior height or velocity rejects a candidate.
Tracking and lifetime Multi-date tracking preserving polarity, followed by a lifetime greater than 30 days. Single-day identification only; no tracking or lifetime filter. Counts cannot reproduce the lifetime-selected census.
