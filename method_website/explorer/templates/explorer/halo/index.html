Inspect the processing steps

{{ core_count }} eight-connected negative-W regions. Candidate counts include only closed loops within the diameter cap; open and oversized contours are excluded before diagnostics.

Step 1 · Compute and smooth Okubo–Weiss W

W combines normal strain, shear strain, and relative vorticity from geostrophic velocities. Negative W indicates rotation-dominated flow. Apply {{ run_settings.filter_passes }} Hanning passes to W, preserving missing cells. SLA is not smoothed or high-pass filtered.

Step 2 · Identify rotating regions

Eight-neighbour connectivity groups W < 0 pixels. Crosses mark each region's minimum-W representative; labels are core IDs, not candidate IDs.

Step 3 · Combine closed SLA loops with rotating cores

Trace loops at {{ run_settings.contour_interval_cm }} cm intervals and keep closed contours with maximum pair distance at most {{ run_settings.max_diameter_km }} km. Require finite interior data and exactly one enclosed core representative, then retain the largest eligible loop for that core.
