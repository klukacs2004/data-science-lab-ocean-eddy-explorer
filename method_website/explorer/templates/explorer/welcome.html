Mesoscale ocean dynamics

Mesoscale Ocean Eddies along the U.S. West Coast

The ocean is a highly dynamic system characterized by mesoscale eddies that play an important role in ocean circulation.

This project investigates mesoscale eddies along the U.S. West Coast using satellite-derived sea-level anomaly and geostrophic velocity data. Three detection methods are compared to examine how they identify eddies and estimate their properties.

Research focus

How are eddy size and lifetime related?

We investigate whether longer-lived eddies tend to grow larger and more rapidly, as observed by Escudier et al. (2016) in the Western Mediterranean Sea.
