Aspect Paper This explorer
Data and sampling Global 0.25° AVISO Reference Series, sampled every 7 days, covering 1992–2008. Daily OSU blended altimetry over the U.S. West Coast. Different source fields, coverage, and cadence prevent direct comparison of catalogue counts.
Background removal Spatial high-pass filtering with cutoff wavelengths of 20° longitude × 10° latitude. Choose a custom Gaussian, a Gaussian approximation calibrated to those scales, or no additional filtering. The custom σ=4 cells gives approximately 4.01° × 4.01° ideal high-pass half-power wavelengths on a 0.25° grid. Coastline normalization and the limited domain alter the response.
Threshold search Both polarities scanned from −100 to +100 cm in 1 cm steps, in opposite directions. Accepted interior pixels are retired separately for each polarity. Preserved in full-scan mode. Single-threshold mode is an exploratory extension and does not identify every outermost acceptable region.
Shape and extrema Simply connected regions with 8–999 pixels and at least one strict local extremum whose four neighbors are inside the region. Uses four-neighbor connectivity and the interior-extremum rule. Multiple extrema are permitted; patches are not automatically split. Extra domain-edge and missing-data guards reject contours that cannot be established on this cropped field.
Amplitude At least 1 cm, measured from the mean SSH of perimeter pixels to the interior extremum. Same pixel-based amplitude definition. The inspector shows the perimeter mean, extremum, and amplitude; it does not substitute the threshold for the perimeter mean.
Compactness All pixel-pair distances below 400 km poleward of 25°, with the limit increasing linearly to 1200 km at the equator. Uses great-circle distances for all pixel pairs. The latitude-dependent limit is evaluated at the candidate's mean pixel latitude. All current U.S. West Coast candidates use 400 km.
Size measures Distinguishes area-equivalent radius L_eff from the speed-based radius L_s and Gaussian scales. Reports pixel area, L_eff, and maximum pair distance. It does not estimate L_s; a compactness distance must not be interpreted as a speed-based eddy diameter.
Tracking and nonlinearity Multi-date tracking and lifetime filtering; main analyses concern tracks of at least 16 weeks and include the nonlinearity metric U/c. No tracking, lifetime filter, or propagation-speed estimate on this page. Passing these snapshot checks does not prove that a patch is a long-lived nonlinear eddy.

Inspect sensitivity and candidate geometry before interpreting the count. Independent labels and a multi-date analysis are needed to evaluate detection accuracy or reproduce the paper's tracked population.
