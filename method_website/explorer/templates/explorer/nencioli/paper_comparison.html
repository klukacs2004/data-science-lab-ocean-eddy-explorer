Aspect Paper This explorer and its effect
Input and resolution The model example uses a 1 km grid; HF radar is also examined. Daily OSU geostrophic velocities on a 0.25° grid. Different flow, dates, and physical neighborhood sizes require new parameter assessment.
Four center constraints EW and NS reversal/growth, a stable local speed minimum, and consistent circulation. Same sequence, with minimum windows at ±b and a rotation perimeter at ±(a−1). Perimeter turning must complete a full revolution. Missing vectors cannot demonstrate circulation.
Streamfunction Average of two trapezoidal integration paths, appendix A1–A4. Same integration principle, using geographic grid distances. A local finite region is expanded or reduced as described in the paper.
Outermost growing contour Outermost closed contour with increasing radial speed at its four extrema. Fractional polygon area ranks nested contours. Speed is sampled half a grid step inside and outside each extremum. This stencil is a numerical choice; its width is not specified in the paper.
Contour resolution No contour-level count is prescribed. 100 levels sample the local streamfunction. Small contours can be sensitive to sampling; this is separate from the method's a and b parameters.
Circular boundaries If no closed contour exists, estimate a circle of radius a−1 grid cells (p. 572). Preserved and labelled as an estimated circle. A contour that fails the growth test is rejected rather than replaced with a circle.
Tracking and validation Multi-day tracking, lifetime filtering, and comparison with manually identified eddies. This page investigates a single day, without tracking or lifetime filtering. It cannot reproduce the paper's success/excess rates without independent labels.

Use a sweep to assess sensitivity, then inspect the velocity geometry. A smaller a can recover smaller features, but features with radii of only one or two grid cells remain poorly resolved. The largest detection count is not a validated optimum.
