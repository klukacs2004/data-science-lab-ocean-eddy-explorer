How tracking works

Each available daily velocity grid is processed independently by the existing Nencioli detector and standardized before tracking. The tracker links the nearest same-polarity center within the daily radius, then attempts a single-day gap within the gap radius. Each detection belongs to at most one track.

Lifetime is the inclusive calendar span from first to last observation, so it can exceed the number of observations when there are gaps. Statistics and the table describe accepted tracks over the full interval; selecting a track only changes the visualization.

Search distances and greedy conflict resolution are practical adaptations of the Nencioli approach. They are not an exact reproduction of the paper's grid-window implementation. Tracks are limited by the selected date interval; a trajectory can begin before or continue after it.
